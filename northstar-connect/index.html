Approved account balances, spending and budgets, income summaries and income transactions, and saved recurring schedules. Amounts stay separated by currency.

Budget limits cover all your accounts; reported spending covers only the accounts you select.

Income rows include date, amount, currency, account, category and identifier. Expense transaction details, notes, receipts, counterparty details and investment holdings are not shared. Income includes recorded income inflows; transfers and refunds are excluded.

Shared expenses use your saved personal share, matching Insights. Split transactions use their stored personal amount. Refunds and transfers are excluded from spending.
